# NEXUS ARCADE v0.10.0 — "NEXUS GREENS"

**Release date:** 2026-10-09 · **Type:** flagship game launch
**New game:** NEXUS GREENS — AAA VR minigolf (replaces Gravity Golf, culled per the cull rule)

## NEXUS GREENS — the showpiece

**18-hole championship course, par 54, 3 themed zones:**
- **Clubhouse Greens** (1–6): tutorial ramp — straight putts, bank shots, pot slalom, brass bridge risk/reward, koi pond, lighthouse loop
- **The Tinkerworks** (7–12): mechanical hazards — counter-rotating gears, piston alley, copper tube corkscrew, opposed conveyors, steam vents, full vertical loop-the-loop
- **The Sky Reef** (13–18): spectacle + wind — visible wind fields, Moonlit Hollow low-gravity patch, teleporter gates, swaying rope bridge, coral maze with starfish gate, storm cell, Skyfall Finale spiral with jump-pad legend line

**Putting feel (lives or dies here):** real putting stroke — no buttons, no meters. Club-head velocity maps to ball launch (0–8 m/s → 0–7 m/s); face angle matters; address mode with dotted aim line; backswing haptic curve (power is felt, never metered). Lip-outs are honest; 6cm funnel assist; per-surface friction.

**Hole-in-one celebration (9 beats, ~10s):** slow-mo drop → zone-themed particle eruption → marquee banner → light sweep → scorecard gold star + Legends Board → slow-mo replay vignette → physical trophy flies to the trophy wall → cheering ghost-avatar. Skippable after 2s; trophy still lands.

**Power-ups (8 holes):** physical glowing charms — spring-hop (clear walls), cup-vacuum (bend near-misses home), sticky ball (kill a too-hot putt). Hand-grab/putter-touch to collect, trigger to activate. One held at a time, single use, no carrying between holes.

**AR ROOM GOLF mode (headline feature):** the course builds from your REAL room via RoomKit — real walls become bank-shot surfaces, real furniture becomes obstacles, "putt under the coffee table" tunnels. Depth occlusion hides virtual behind real. 5-hole **Home Tour** bonus loop (AR-exclusive holes: Off Your Wall, Under the Coffee Table, Around the Couch, Chair Slalom, Kitchen Island Rail). Safety: guardian boundary, ball auto-return, stance clearances. Scan onboarding built in.

**Full sound:** 12 original synthesized SFX (putt thock, cup rattle, fanfare, crowd swell/gasp, rail clicks, power-up sounds, wind) + 13 haptic sequences.

## Cull
- **Gravity Golf** removed outright (single flat hole, no progression — not amazing). Its portal/gravity-well mechanics graduate into NEXUS GREENS holes 14 + Sky Reef wind. 75 experiences remain.
